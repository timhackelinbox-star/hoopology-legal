2. Überblick und Grundsatz der Datensparsamkeit

Hoopology ist eine App für Basketball-Fans zum Dokumentieren von Spielbesuchen, Sammeln von Arenen, Verdienen von Abzeichen und Vernetzen mit anderen Fans. Wir verarbeiten personenbezogene Daten nur, soweit dies für den Betrieb der App erforderlich ist oder du eingewilligt hast.

Wir legen Wert auf Datensparsamkeit. Insbesondere:

  • Wir speichern keine rohen Standortdaten deines Geräts.
  • Wir speichern keine IP-Adressen in unseren Anwendungs-Logs.
  • An unseren Analyse-Dienst (PostHog) übermitteln wir nur mit deiner Einwilligung pseudonyme Ereignisdaten und allgemeine Gerätedaten, niemals deinen Namen, deine E-Mail-Adresse oder deine Koordinaten.
  • Der Großteil deiner Daten wird in der Europäischen Union (Frankfurt, Deutschland) verarbeitet.

Profilbild: Du kannst freiwillig ein Profilbild aus deiner Fotomediathek wählen oder mit der Kamera aufnehmen. Es wird vor dem Hochladen auf deinem Gerät quadratisch zugeschnitten und auf 512 × 512 Pixel verkleinert; Metadaten des Fotos wie der Aufnahmeort werden dabei entfernt. Dein Profilbild ist für andere Nutzer der App sichtbar und hat eine eigene Internetadresse: Wer diese Adresse kennt, kann das Bild auch ohne Anmeldung öffnen. Eine Liste aller gespeicherten Profilbilder lässt sich nicht abrufen. Ersetzt du dein Bild, löschen wir das alte. d) Standortdaten (Sonderfall)

Mit deiner Erlaubnis greifen wir auf die Position deines Geräts zu, nur während du die App nutzt und nie im Hintergrund, und zwar in diesen Situationen:

  • auf dem Startbildschirm, um dir einen Check-in vorzuschlagen, wenn du dich in einer Arena mit einem laufenden oder bald beginnenden Spiel befindest;
  • im Spieldetail eines laufenden oder bald beginnenden Spiels, um dir dort den Check-in anzubieten;
  • beim GPS-Check-in, um zu prüfen, ob du dich in der Arena des Spiels befindest; dafür übermitteln wir deine Position zusammen mit der von deinem Gerät gemeldeten Genauigkeit;
  • in der Umgebungssuche, um Spiele in deiner Nähe zu finden; statt deiner Position kannst du auch einen Ort auf der Karte wählen, dann wird keine Geräteposition verwendet.

Die Position wird an unseren Server übermittelt und dort nur für die jeweilige Berechnung verwendet. Wir speichern sie weder auf deinem Gerät noch auf unserem Server, protokollieren sie nicht und geben sie nicht an Dritte weiter. Beim Check-in speichern wir nur das Spiel und die Arena, nicht deine Position. Die Schaltfläche „Mein Standort“ auf der Karte verschiebt nur den Kartenausschnitt auf deinem Gerät; dabei geht deine Position nicht an unseren Server (zu den Kartenanfragen an Mapbox siehe Abschnitt 4). Ohne Standortfreigabe kannst du vergangene Spiele weiterhin manuell nachtragen; bei laufenden und bald beginnenden Spielen ist ein Check-in nur vor Ort möglich.

Benachrichtigungen betreffen ausschließlich deine Freunde in der App: neue und angenommene Freundschaftsanfragen, Check-ins von Freunden und Freunde beim selben Spiel. Hinweise auf Freunde beim selben Spiel senden wir auch während deiner Ruhezeiten, weil sie nur in diesem Moment nützlich sind; du kannst sie in den Einstellungen einzeln abschalten. Für die Zustellung nutzen wir den Push-Dienst von Expo (siehe Abschnitt 4) und den Benachrichtigungsdienst deines Geräteherstellers (bei iOS den Apple Push Notification service). f) Melden und Blockieren

Du kannst andere Nutzer melden oder blockieren. Bei einer Meldung speichern wir, wer gemeldet hat, wer gemeldet wurde, den gewählten Grund (aus einer festen Liste, ohne Freitext) und den Zeitpunkt. Wir prüfen jede Meldung von Hand; die gemeldete Person erfährt nicht, wer sie gemeldet hat. Stellen wir keinen Verstoß fest, löschen wir die Meldung nach der Prüfung. Bei einem Verstoß bewahren wir sie auf, um wiederholte Verstöße zu erkennen, längstens bis zur Löschung des meldenden oder des gemeldeten Kontos. Beim Blockieren speichern wir die Blockierung (blockierende und blockierte Person, Zeitpunkt). Nach einer Blockierung sind die Profile füreinander nicht mehr sichtbar, eine bestehende Freundschaft wird beendet, und es werden keine Benachrichtigungen mehr übereinander versendet; die blockierte Person wird darüber nicht informiert. Blockierungen speichern wir bis zur Aufhebung oder bis zur Löschung eines der beiden Konten.

Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO (berechtigtes Interesse am Schutz unserer Nutzer und an einer sicheren Community).

g) Technische Server-Logs und Überlastungsschutz

Bei Anfragen an unsere Server protokollieren wir technische Eckdaten: Anfrage-Methode, den aufgerufenen Endpunkt in allgemeiner Form (zum Beispiel „/users/:id/profile“, ohne die konkrete Kennung), Statuscode, Antwortzeit, Zeitstempel und eine Anfrage-Kennung zur Fehlerkorrelation. Wird ein GPS-Check-in abgelehnt, weil du nicht in der Arena bist, protokollieren wir zusätzlich nur eine grobe Entfernungsstufe (zum Beispiel „1 bis 5 km“), ohne Position, Nutzer, Arena oder Spiel. Wir protokollieren keine IP-Adressen, keine Inhalte und keine Standortparameter. Die Logs liegen bei unserem Hosting-Anbieter Render und werden nach 7 Tagen automatisch gelöscht.

Zum Schutz vor Missbrauch und Überlastung begrenzen wir, wie viele Anfragen pro Minute unseren Server erreichen: bei angemeldeter Nutzung je Konto, bei Anfragen ohne Anmeldung (zum Beispiel Spielpläne und Hallen) und bei fehlgeschlagenen Anmeldeprüfungen je IP-Adresse. Dafür werden Konto-Kennung bzw. IP-Adresse kurzzeitig im Arbeitsspeicher des Servers gezählt; die Zähler verfallen nach spätestens einer Minute. Eine dauerhafte Speicherung oder Protokollierung findet nicht statt.

Rechtsgrundlage: Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO (berechtigtes Interesse am sicheren, stabilen und fehlerfreien Betrieb).

2. Overview and Data Minimization

Hoopology is an app for basketball fans to document game attendance, collect arenas, earn badges, and connect with other fans. We process personal data only insofar as necessary to operate the app or where you have given consent.

We value data minimization. In particular:

  • We do not store your device's raw location data.
  • We do not store IP addresses in our application logs.
  • We send pseudonymous event data and general device data to our analytics service (PostHog) only with your consent, never your name, email address or coordinates.
  • The majority of your data is processed in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

3. What Data We Process

a) Account and identity data

Upon registration and use, we process: your email address and (encrypted) password for authentication, your username, and voluntary details such as display name, biography, profile picture, favorite team, and your privacy setting.

Profile picture: You can optionally choose a profile picture from your photo library or take one with your camera. Before upload, it is cropped to a square and downscaled to 512 × 512 pixels on your device; photo metadata such as the location where it was taken is removed in the process. We store it in our file storage at Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, EU). Your profile picture is visible to other users of the app and has its own web address: anyone who knows this address can open the picture without signing in. A list of all stored profile pictures cannot be retrieved. When you replace your picture, we delete the old one. You can remove it from your profile at any time; when your account is deleted, all your profile pictures are deleted.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract) for data necessary for account management; Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent) for voluntary profile details.

b) Sign in with Apple or Google (optional)

Instead of email and password, you can sign in with your Apple or Google account. Hoopology then receives a sign-in token and your email address from the provider (with Apple, optionally an anonymised relay address); further details supplied by the provider (with Google, for example your name and your Google profile picture) are not taken over into your Hoopology profile but remain in the sign-in data held by our authentication provider and are deleted together with your account. Token verification and account management are handled by our authentication provider Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, EU). Apple or Google learn that you use Hoopology when you sign in; their privacy policies apply in addition. If a Hoopology account with the same verified email address already exists, the sign-in is linked to that account; no second account is created.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract — providing the sign-in method you chose).

c) Usage and content data

We process your check-ins (visited arena, game, time, mode, matchup), earned badges, and your friend relationships (requests, status). This data forms the core of the app's functionality.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

d) Location data (special case)

With your permission, we access your device's position only while you are using the app and never in the background, in the following situations:

  • on the home screen, to suggest a check-in when you are at an arena with a game that is live or about to start;
  • in the game details of a game that is live or about to start, to offer you a check-in there;
  • during a GPS check-in, to verify that you are at the arena of the game; for this we transmit your position together with the accuracy reported by your device;
  • in the nearby search, to find games close to you; instead of your position you can also pick a place on the map, in which case no device position is used.

The position is sent to our server and used there only for the respective calculation. We do not store it on your device or on our server, we do not log it, and we do not share it with third parties. For a check-in we only store the game and the arena, not your position. The “My location” button on the map only moves the map view on your device; your position is not sent to our server (for map requests to Mapbox, see section 4). Without location access you can still add past games manually; for games that are live or about to start, a check-in is only possible on site.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent via your operating system's location permission, which you can revoke at any time).

e) Push notifications (optional)

If you enable push notifications, we process a device token (an identifier for your device issued by the notification service), the platform, the app version, your device's language and time zone, and a randomly generated installation identifier. This lets us send messages to the right device, in your language and in line with your quiet hours. Your notification settings (categories, quiet hours) are stored in your account. Notifications only concern your friends in the app: new and accepted friend requests, friends checking in, and friends at the same game. We also send notifications about friends at the same game during your quiet hours, because they are only useful at that moment; you can turn them off individually in the settings. For delivery we use Expo's push service (see section 4) and your device manufacturer's notification service (on iOS, the Apple Push Notification service). The device token and the content of the message are transmitted for this purpose, for example a friend's display name and the game pairing. We keep a log of sent notifications (recipient, triggering user, category, title and text, destination in the app, delivery status, technical delivery data such as ticket identifiers and error codes, times of sending and opening). Entries older than 90 days are deleted automatically; when your account is deleted, all entries in which you are the recipient or the triggering user are deleted. When you sign out, we delete the device token; if your device is offline at that moment, we do so the next time the app starts.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent via your operating system's notification permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings or in the app).

f) Reporting and blocking

You can report or block other users. For a report we store who reported, who was reported, the selected reason (from a fixed list, no free text) and the time. We review every report by hand; the reported person does not learn who reported them. If we find no violation, we delete the report after the review. If there is a violation, we keep it to detect repeat violations, at the longest until the reporting or the reported account is deleted. For a block we store the block (blocking and blocked user, time). After a block, the two profiles are no longer visible to each other, an existing friendship is ended, and no notifications about each other are sent; the blocked person is not informed. We store blocks until they are lifted or until either of the two accounts is deleted.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in protecting our users and keeping the community safe).

g) Technical server logs and overload protection

When requests are made to our servers, we log technical metadata: request method, the accessed endpoint in generic form (for example “/users/:id/profile”, without the actual identifier), status code, response time, timestamp and a request identifier for error correlation. If a GPS check-in is rejected because you are not at the arena, we additionally log only a rough distance band (for example “1 to 5 km”), without position, user, arena or game. We do not log IP addresses, content or location parameters. The logs are kept by our hosting provider Render and deleted automatically after 7 days.

To protect against abuse and overload, we limit how many requests per minute reach our server: per account for signed-in use, and per IP address for requests without sign-in (for example schedules and arenas) and for failed sign-in checks. For this, the account identifier or IP address is counted briefly in the server's memory; the counters expire after one minute at the latest. Nothing is stored permanently or logged.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, stable and error-free operation).

4. Recipients and Services Used (Processors)

Render (Render Services, Inc., USA): operation of our application server. Server location: Frankfurt, Germany (EU). The app's requests are processed here and the technical server logs are stored here (see 3g); position data is neither stored nor logged.

Resend (Resend, Inc., USA): sending of sign-in emails, i.e. the confirmation code at registration and the code for resetting your password. Mapbox (Mapbox, Inc., USA): rendering of maps in the app. To display the map, the app requests map data for the visible area from Mapbox. In doing so, Mapbox technically receives your IP address and device data (device model, operating system, an app-specific device identifier for billing). We do not transmit your device position to Mapbox; however, if the map in the nearby search jumps to your position, the approximate area can be inferred from the map data requested. The app draws the arenas itself; their data is not sent to Mapbox. We have turned off the usage statistics of the map SDK (telemetry); you can turn them back on yourself via the info symbol (i) on the map. Transfers to the USA are based on appropriate safeguards (EU Standard Contractual Clauses). Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a functioning map view).

PostHog: product analytics to improve the app, only if you have consented. We transmit pseudonymous event data (for example which feature was used, categories, numbers) as well as general device data (device model, operating system, app version, language, time zone); your user ID is replaced by a cryptographic hash. No name, email address or coordinates are transmitted. PostHog does not store your IP address and does not derive a location from it. Event data is deleted after one year. Server location: EU. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent); see section 5.

Sentry: detection of software errors and crashes to ensure stability. Technical error data is transmitted: device model, operating system, app version, a random installation identifier, a pseudonymous device identifier and the last steps in the app before the error. We do not transmit your name or email address, and the current app version does not transmit search terms or location data either. Older test versions of the app (up to build 17) may still transmit search terms, location data, internal identifiers and the username shown in the block dialog when an error occurs; in that case Sentry removes or replaces them on receipt, before the data is stored. Sentry does not store IP addresses for the app. Error data is deleted after 90 days at the latest. Server location: Germany (EU). Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a stable app).

Expo (650 Industries, Inc., USA): delivery of push notifications via the Expo Push Service. The device token and the content of each message are transmitted; Expo forwards them to the device manufacturer's notification service. Transfers to the USA are based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent); see section 3e.

Apple (Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland): delivery of push notifications on iOS devices via the Apple Push Notification service and, if you use it, Sign in with Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies in addition. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (push, see section 3e) or (b) GDPR (sign-in, see section 3b).

Google (Google Ireland Ltd., Ireland): Sign in with Google, if you use it. Google's privacy policy applies in addition. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract); see section 3b.

5. Consent to Product Analytics (Opt-in)

Product analytics (PostHog) is disabled by default. It only becomes active if you expressly consent. You can revoke your consent at any time in the app settings under "Data"; revocation takes effect for the future. Until consent is given, no analytics events are sent to PostHog.

6. Retention and Deletion

We store your account and usage data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account yourself at any time in the settings under “Delete account”. We then immediately and permanently delete all data linked to your account in our database and file storage: your check-ins, badges, both sides of your friendships, blocks and reports by you and about you, your profile pictures, device tokens, notification settings and notifications, your profile and your sign-in account.

Some data is not stored in our database and is therefore not deleted immediately:

  • Pseudonymous analytics data at PostHog (only if you had consented) and technical error data at Sentry remain stored until their respective retention period ends (PostHog one year, Sentry at most 90 days). They contain neither your name nor your email address and can no longer be linked to you once your account is deleted.
  • If a friend received a “friends at the same game” notification that mentions your display name, it remains in their notification log until it is deleted automatically after 90 days at the latest.
  • If you signed in with Apple or Google, the connection with that provider remains. You can remove it in your Apple ID settings under “Sign in with Apple” or in your Google Account under connected third-party apps.

We also delete device tokens when you sign out or when your device reports the token as invalid. We delete entries in the notification log after 90 days, and reports without a violation after the review (see 3f). Our hosting provider deletes server logs after 7 days; they contain no IP addresses and no identifiers (see 3g). Location data is not stored (see 3d).
